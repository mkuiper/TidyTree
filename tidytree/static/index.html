TidyTree Help & Configuration Guide

Taxonomy Reference Guide

Generic (File Types)

Default format classification: Groups into Documents, Data & Sheets, Media, Source Code, and Archives.

Government Agency

Tailored public administration sectors: Policy & Legislation, Operations & Services, Finance & Procurement, Administration & HR, and Communications & Relations.

Corporate Organization

Standard business operational segments: Finance & Legal, Human Resources, Marketing & Sales, Product & Operations, and Engineering & Tech.

Academic / School

Optimized research structure: Research & Publications, Teaching & Courses, Administration & Departmental, and Student Portfolios.

Feature Walkthrough

  • Search Bar: Filters files dynamically in both directories. Non-matching nodes are faded, matching filenames are highlighted, and parent folders auto-expand to reveal matches.
  • Symbolic Metrics: Compares file count, folder count, and maximum nesting depth side-by-side, displaying a visual bar chart of folder category weights.
  • AI Reorganization: If GEMINI_API_KEY or OPENAI_API_KEY is set in the .env file (or inputs), the engine utilizes Large Language Models to classify files matching your custom guidance context.
